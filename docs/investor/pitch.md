# SokogateOS — Investor Pitch

## Elevator Pitch

**SokogateOS is an AI operating system that makes African businesses legible to AI by default, turning their company artifacts into self-improving loops for sourcing, customization, and logistics.**

---

## Problem

- **$1.5T+** African B2B commerce market — yet 80%+ of transactions still happen offline via phone calls, WhatsApp, and in-person meetings
- **No standardized data** — companies have fragmented documents, emails, spreadsheets with no unified structure
- **AI cannot help** — because the data isn't structured, AI/ML tools are useless to most African businesses
- **Procurement is broken** — finding reliable suppliers, negotiating prices, managing logistics across borders is incredibly manual and opaque

## Solution

**SokogateOS** solves this by:

1. **Ingesting** every company artifact (documents, emails, spreadsheets, voice notes) through our AI Legibility Layer
2. **Structuring** that data into AI-understandable formats with knowledge graphs
3. **Powering** three core services: sourcing, customization, and logistics
4. **Self-improving** — every action generates feedback that trains the AI engine continuously
