## Key Assumptions

1. **Market growth:** African B2B e-commerce growing at 25% CAGR
2. **Customer acquisition:** Inbound + partnerships with trade associations
3. **Churn:** <5% monthly for enterprise accounts
4. **Expansion revenue:** 20% quarterly from add-on services
5. **Infrastructure costs:** Scaling linearly with transaction volume
