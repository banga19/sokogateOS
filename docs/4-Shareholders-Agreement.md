## Section 2: Transfer Restrictions
### 2.1 Right of First Refusal (ROFR)
Before any Shareholder may Transfer any Shares to any person who is not a Permitted Transferee, such Shareholder shall first offer such Shares to the Company and the other Shareholders.

#### Procedure:
1. Selling Shareholder provides written notice to Company and other Shareholders detailing:
   - Number of Shares to be transferred
   - Price per Share
   - Identity of proposed transferee
   - Material terms of proposed transfer
2. Company has 30 days to elect to purchase Shares
3. If Company declines or partially purchases, other Shareholders have 30 days to purchase remaining Shares pro rata
4. If not purchased, Selling Shareholder may Transfer to proposed transferee on same terms within 60 days

### 2.2 Tag-Along Rights
If a Shareholder receives a bona fide offer to purchase Shares representing 50% or more of the Company's voting power, such Shareholder shall notify other Shareholders who shall have the right to participate in such sale on the same terms and conditions.

### 2.3 Drag-Along Rights
If Shareholders holding 75% or more of the Company's voting power approve a sale of the Company, such Shareholders may require all other Shareholders to sell their Shares on the same terms and conditions in connection with such sale.

### 2.4 Permitted Transfers
Transfers to the following are exempt from ROFR, tag-along, and drag-along provisions:
- Affiliates of the transferring Shareholder
- Transfers to trusts for estate planning purposes where the Shareholder remains beneficiary
- Transfers pursuant to divorce settlements or separation agreements
- Transfers to heirs upon death of Shareholder (subject to Section 2.5)

### 2.5 Death or Incapacity
Upon the death or legal incapacity of a Shareholder:
- The estate, heirs, or legal representative shall have 90 days to elect to sell the Shares
- The Company and remaining Shareholders shall have ROFR on such Shares
- If not purchased, Shares may be transferred to estate planning vehicles or family members

## Section 3: Governance and Voting
### 3.1 Board of Directors
- The Board shall consist of 3 directors initially
- Founders shall each have the right to nominate 1 director
- The third director shall be mutually agreed upon by both Founders
- Additional directors shall be appointed by majority vote of Shareholders
- Directors shall serve 3-year terms

### 3.2 Shareholder Meetings
- Annual meetings shall be held each year for election of directors and other business
- Special meetings may be called by:
  - Board of Directors
  - Shareholders holding 25% or more of voting shares
- Notice shall be given 20 days prior to meetings
- Quorum shall be 33.3% of voting shares

### 3.3 Voting Rights
- Each Share entitles holder to one vote on all matters
- Founders shall have special voting rights as follows:
  - Founders have veto power over any amendment to the Certificate of Incorporation or Bylaws
- Reserved matters requiring 75% supermajority vote:
  - Amendment to Certificate of Incorporation or Bylaws
  - Authorization of additional shares
  - Incurrence of debt exceeding $1,000,000
  - Approval of annual budget
  - Declaration of dividends
  - Merger, consolidation, or sale of substantially all assets
  - Related party transactions

## Section 4: Information Rights
### 4.1 Financial Reporting
The Company shall provide to all Shareholders:
- Annual audited financial statements within 90 days of fiscal year end
- Quarterly unaudited financial statements within 45 days of quarter end
- Annual operating budget prior to fiscal year start
- Material event notifications within 5 days of occurrence

### 4.2 Inspection Rights
Shareholders holding 10% or more of shares shall have the right to:
- Inspect corporate books and records during business hours
- Discuss affairs of the Company with officers and directors
- Receive copies of minutes of Board and Shareholder meetings

## Section 5: Founder-Specific Provisions
### 5.1 Founder Vesting
All Founder Shares are subject to vesting schedule as outlined in the Founder Agreement:
- 4-year vesting with 1-year cliff
- 25% vests after 12 months
- Remaining 75% vests monthly thereafter

### 5.2 Founder Departure
Upon departure of a Founder:
- Vested Shares remain with Founder (subject to Sections 2.1-2.5)
- Unvested Shares are subject to repurchase by Company at Original Purchase Price
- Founder shall comply with all confidentiality, non-compete, and non-solicitation obligations

## Section 6: Drag-Along and Tag-Along Procedures
### 6.1 Drag-Along Exercise
- Majority Shareholders must provide written notice to all Shareholders
- Notice must include:
  - Identity of proposed buyer
  - Purchase price per Share
  - Material terms of proposed transaction
  - Closing date and conditions
- Minority Shareholders must execute all necessary documents and deliver Shares at closing

### 6.2 Tag-Along Exercise
- Selling Shareholder must provide written notice to all Shareholders
- Other Shareholders have 30 days to elect to participate
- Participating Shareholders must notify Selling Shareholder of number of Shares to be sold
- All selling Shareholders shall sell on identical terms and conditions
