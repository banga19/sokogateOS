## 1. Definition of Confidential Information
For purposes of this Agreement, "Confidential Information" shall mean all information or material that has or could have commercial value or other utility in the business in which Disclosing Party is engaged. If Confidential Information is in written form, the Disclosing Party shall label or stamp the materials with the word "Confidential" or some similar warning. If Confidential Information is transmitted orally, the Disclosing Party shall promptly provide a written indication that such oral communication constituted Confidential Information.

Confidential Information includes, but is not limited to:
- Research, product plans, products, services, designs, drawings, engineering, hardware, software, firmware, source codes, and related documentation
- Financial information, pricing, cost structures, profit margins, and business plans
- Marketing strategies, customer lists, supplier information, and trade secrets
- Technical data, test results, algorithms, databases, and proprietary processes
- Personnel information, organizational structure, and business relationships
- Any analyses, compilations, studies, or other documents prepared by Receiving Party based on Confidential Information

Confidential Information shall not include information that:
- Is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement by Receiving Party
- Was rightfully in Receiving Party's possession without restriction prior to disclosure
- Is rightfully obtained by Receiving Party from a third party without restriction and without breach of any obligation to Disclosing Party
- Is independently developed by Receiving Party without use of or reference to Disclosing Party's Confidential Information
- Is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order (provided Receiving Party gives Disclosing Party prompt notice and opportunity to seek protective relief)

## 2. Obligations of Receiving Party
Receiving Party shall:
- Maintain Confidential Information in strict confidence using at least the same degree of care as it uses to protect its own confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care
- Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without Disclosing Party's prior written consent
- Use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of evaluating or engaging in a potential business relationship with Disclosing Party ("Purpose")
- Limit access to Confidential Information to employees, contractors, and agents who have a need to know and who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less restrictive than this Agreement
- Notify Disclosing Party promptly of any unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information
- Return or destroy all Confidential Information upon termination of discussions or upon Disclosing Party's request

## 3. Term
The confidentiality obligations herein shall continue for three (3) years from the date this Agreement is signed, except with respect to Trade Secrets, which shall remain protected for as long as such information qualifies as a trade secret under applicable law.

## 4. No License
Nothing in this Agreement grants Receiving Party any rights, by implication, estoppel, or otherwise, to any intellectual property rights of Disclosing Party, including any patent, copyright, trademark, or trade secret.

## 5. Return of Materials
Upon termination of discussions or upon Disclosing Party's request, Receiving Party shall:
- Promptly return all tangible materials containing Confidential Information
- Destroy all electronic copies and certify destruction in writing
- Retain only one archival copy for legal compliance purposes, subject to continued confidentiality obligations
