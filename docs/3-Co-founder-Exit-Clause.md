# Co-founder Exit Clause

## Between:
- **Bangali Fofana**, Chief Technology Officer (CTO)
- **Amadou Balde**, Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

## Effective Date: June 13, 2026

## Purpose
This Co-founder Exit Clause ("Agreement") sets forth the terms and conditions under which a Founder may depart from sokogateOS (the "Company") and addresses the treatment of equity, intellectual property, and ongoing obligations.

## Definitions
- **"Founder"**: Refers to either Bangali Fofana or Amadou Balde
- **"Good Leaver"**: A Founder who departs due to:
  - Death or permanent disability
  - Termination by the Company without Cause
  - Resignation for Good Reason (as defined below)
- **"Bad Leaver"**: A Founder who departs due to:
  - Termination by the Company for Cause
  - Resignation without Good Reason
  - Breach of this Agreement or other material agreements with the Company
- **"Cause"**: Means:
  - Conviction of a felony or crime involving moral turpitude
  - Material breach of fiduciary duties
  - Gross negligence or willful misconduct
  - Material violation of Company policies
  - Failure to perform duties after written notice and cure period
- **"Good Reason"**: Means:
  - Material reduction in responsibilities, authority, or reporting lines
  - Relocation requirement exceeding 50 miles from current location
  - Material reduction in compensation or benefits
  - Material change in ownership or control without Founder's consent
  - Failure to obtain required approvals for Founder's continued service

## Exit Triggers and Equity Treatment

### 1. Voluntary Departure (Resignation)
A Founder may voluntarily resign by providing 30 days' written notice to the other Founder and the Board.

#### Good Leaver Resignation:
- **Vested Shares**: Retain all vested shares
- **Unvested Shares**: Forfeit all unvested shares (subject to acceleration provisions below)
- **Stock Options**: May exercise vested options within 90 days of departure
- **Acceleration**: Unvested shares accelerate if resignation is for Good Reason

#### Bad Leaver Resignation:
- **Vested Shares**: May be subject to repurchase by Company at Fair Market Value
- **Unvested Shares**: Forfeited immediately
- **Stock Options**: All options (vested and unvested) forfeited immediately

### 2. Termination by Company
The Company may terminate a Founder's employment with or without Cause.

#### Termination Without Cause (Good Leaver):
- **Vested Shares**: Retain all vested shares
- **Unvested Shares**: 100% acceleration of unvested shares
- **Stock Options**: May exercise vested options within 90 days
- **Severance**: 6 months' base salary continuation + benefits

#### Termination For Cause (Bad Leaver):
- **Vested Shares**: Subject to repurchase at LOWER OF Fair Market Value or Original Purchase Price
- **Unvested Shares**: Forfeited immediately
- **Stock Options**: All options forfeited immediately
- **Severance**: None

### 3. Death or Permanent Disability
- **Vested Shares**: Retain all vested shares (transfer to estate or beneficiaries)
- **Unvested Shares**: 100% acceleration of unvested shares
- **Stock Options**: May exercise vested options within 90 days
- **Benefits**: Continued per Company policy and applicable law

## Intellectual Property and Confidentiality Obligations

### 1. IP Assignment
All intellectual property related to the Company's business, whether conceived before or after service, remains assigned to the Company upon departure.

### 2. Confidentiality
Confidentiality obligations survive departure indefinitely and include:
- Technical information
- Business plans and strategies
- Customer and supplier lists
- Financial information
- Personnel information

### 3. Non-Compete
During employment and for 12 months thereafter, Founder shall not:
- Directly or indirectly engage in any business that competes with the Company
- solicit Company employees, contractors, or consultants
- solicit Company customers with whom Founder had material contact

### 4. Non-Solicitation
For 12 months after departure, Founder shall not solicit:
- Company employees, contractors, or consultants
- Company customers or vendors
- Company investors or advisors

## Post-Departure Cooperation

### 1. Transition Assistance
Departing Founder agrees to provide reasonable transition assistance for 4 weeks, including:
- Documentation of responsibilities
- Training of successor
- Introduction to key contacts
- Availability for questions

### 2. Advisory Role (Optional)
Upon mutual agreement, Departing Founder may serve as an advisor for 3 months at $1,000 per month.
