## 1. Equal Employment Opportunity
sokogateOS is committed to providing equal employment opportunities to all employees and applicants without regard to race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, genetic information, marital status, or veteran status. This policy applies to all aspects of employment, including recruitment, hiring, promotion, termination, compensation, and training.

- **Commitment:** We build a diverse and inclusive workplace where all employees feel valued and respected
- **Compliance:** We comply with all applicable federal, state, and local equal employment opportunity laws
- **Reporting:** Employees may report concerns to their supervisor, HR, or through our anonymous reporting channel

## 2. Anti-Harassment and Discrimination
We maintain a workplace free from harassment and discrimination based on any protected characteristic.

### 2.1 Prohibited Conduct
- Harassment includes unwelcome verbal, physical, or visual conduct based on protected characteristics
- Discrimination includes adverse employment actions based on protected characteristics
- Retaliation against employees who report concerns or participate in investigations is strictly prohibited

### 2.2 Reporting Procedures
- Employees should report concerns to their supervisor, HR representative, or designated compliance officer
- Reports may be made anonymously through our ethics hotline
- All reports will be investigated promptly, thoroughly, and impartially
- Confidentiality will be maintained to the extent possible while conducting a thorough investigation

### 2.3 Investigation and Resolution
- Investigations will be conducted by trained personnel
- Appropriate remedial action will be taken when violations are found
- False complaints made in bad faith may result in disciplinary action

## 3. Recruitment and Hiring
Our recruitment and hiring processes are designed to attract qualified candidates while ensuring fairness and compliance.

### 3.1 Job Postings
- All job postings will accurately describe the position's essential functions, qualifications, and responsibilities
- Postings will include our equal employment opportunity statement
- We will use diverse recruitment channels to reach a broad candidate pool

### 3.2 Interview Process
- All interviewers will receive training on legal and effective interviewing techniques
- Interview questions will be job-related and consistent for all candidates applying for the same position
- We will assess candidates based on qualifications, experience, and cultural fit

### 3.3 Selection and Offer
- Hiring decisions will be based on job-related criteria
- All offers will be contingent upon successful completion of pre-employment screening
- We maintain records of hiring decisions to demonstrate compliance with EEO requirements

## 4. Onboarding and Orientation
We provide a structured onboarding process to help new employees succeed.

### 4.1 Pre-Start Date
- Complete all required paperwork (tax forms, employment eligibility verification, etc.)
- Set up IT access and equipment
- Assign an onboarding buddy or mentor

### 4.2 First Day and Week
- Welcome session with HR and team introduction
- Review of company policies, benefits, and safety procedures

- Introduction to company culture, mission, and values

### 4.3 Ongoing Support
- Regular check-ins with supervisor during the first 90 days
- Participation in new employee training programs
- Feedback collection to improve the onboarding experience

## 9. Remote Work and Flexible Arrangements
We offer flexible work arrangements where feasible and beneficial to both employees and the company.

### 9.1 Eligibility
- Positions are evaluated for remote work suitability based on job responsibilities, performance needs, and equipment requirements
- Not all positions are suitable for remote work arrangements
- Eligibility determined by supervisor in consultation with HR

### 9.2 Types of Arrangements
- **Full Remote:** Work performed entirely from an approved remote location
- **Hybrid:** Combination of remote and in-office work according to a set schedule
- **Flexible Hours:** Variable start and end times within established parameters
- **Compressed Workweek:** Standard hours completed in fewer days per week

### 9.3 Requirements and Expectations
- Remote workers must maintain a safe and productive work environment
- Equipment and technology provided or approved by company
- Regular communication and availability during core hours
- Participation in team meetings and company events as required
- Adherence to all company policies while working remotely

### 9.4 Agreement and Review
- Formal remote work agreement outlining terms and expectations
- Regular review of arrangement effectiveness
- Either party may terminate or modify the arrangement with advance notice

## 10. Employee Conduct and Discipline
We expect all employees to maintain professional conduct that reflects positively on themselves and the company.

### 10.1 Standards of Conduct
- Professionalism in all interactions with colleagues, customers, and vendors
- Respect for company property and resources
- Compliance with all company policies and procedures
- Honesty and integrity in all business dealings
- Protection of confidential and proprietary information

### 10.2 Progressive Discipline
When conduct or performance issues arise, we generally follow a progressive discipline approach:
1. Informal coaching and feedback
2. Verbal warning (documented)
3. Written warning
4. Performance improvement plan
5. Suspension (with or without pay)
6. Termination of employment

*Serious violations may warrant immediate termination without progressive discipline.*

### 10.3 Examples of Misconduct
- Violations of company policies
- Insubordination or refusal to follow reasonable instructions
- Dishonesty, fraud, or theft
- Harassment, discrimination, or creating a hostile work environment
- Violence or threats of violence in the workplace
- Substance abuse impairing job performance
- Excessive absenteeism or tardiness
- Unauthorized use of company resources
- Breach of confidentiality

## 11. Health and Safety
We are committed to providing a safe and healthy work environment for all employees.

### 11.1 Workplace Safety
- Compliance with OSHA and equivalent state occupational safety regulations
- Regular safety inspections and hazard assessments
- Provision of necessary safety equipment and protective gear
- Safety training for all employees
- Emergency procedures and evacuation plans

### 11.2 Injury Reporting and Workers' Compensation
- All workplace injuries or illnesses must be reported immediately to supervisor
- Prompt medical attention for work-related injuries
- Workers' compensation benefits provided as required by law
- Return-to-work programs to facilitate recovery and reintegration

### 11.3 Substance-Free Workplace
- Prohibition of illegal drugs and alcohol in the workplace
- Prohibition of reporting to work under the influence of substances that impair job performance
- Drug testing as permitted by law and for safety-sensitive positions
- Employee assistance program available for substance abuse concerns

### 11.4 Ergonomics and Workstation Safety
- Ergonomic assessments available for office employees
- Proper equipment and furniture to prevent repetitive strain injuries
- Training on proper lifting techniques and workstation setup
- Accommodations for employees with disabilities

### 11.5 Visitor and Contractor Safety
- Safety protocols for visitors and contractors on company premises
- Required safety orientation and supervision
- Compliance with company safety policies and procedures

## 14. Separation and Offboarding
We handle employee separations with professionalism, respect, and compliance with legal requirements.

### 14.1 Types of Separation
- Voluntary resignation
- Retirement
- Layoff or reduction in force
- Termination for cause
- Termination without cause
- Death or disability

### 14.2 Notice Requirements
- **Voluntary Resignation:** [NOTICE PERIOD - e.g., two weeks] written notice standard
- **Retirement:** Advance notice as mutually agreed
- **Layoff:** Notice as required by WARN Act or equivalent state law
- **Termination:** Notice may or may not be provided depending on circumstances

### 14.3 Offboarding Process
- Exit interview to gather feedback and insights
- Return of all company property (equipment, access cards, documents, etc.)
- Access termination to systems, email, and facilities
- Benefits continuation information (COBRA or equivalent)
- Final paycheck processing including accrued vacation payout
- Provision of separation documents and tax forms

### 14.4 Post-Employment Obligations
- Continuing confidentiality obligations
- Non-compete and non-solicitation agreements (if applicable)
- Reference checking procedures
- Alumni network opportunities

## 15. Record Keeping and Confidentiality
We maintain accurate employee records while protecting sensitive information.

### 15.1 Personnel Files
- Official personnel files maintained by HR
- Contents include: application materials, offer letter, employment contracts, performance reviews, disciplinary records, training records, and separation documents
- Access restricted to authorized HR personnel and management with legitimate need
- Employees may review their own personnel files upon request
- Retention period: [RETENTION PERIOD - e.g., 7 years] after separation as required by law

### 15.2 Payroll and Tax Records
- Maintenance of payroll records, tax filings, and related documents
- Retention period as required by tax authorities and labor laws
- Secure storage and access controls

### 15.3 Medical and Confidential Records
- Separate, confidential storage for medical information, disability records, and investigation files
- Access limited to authorized personnel only
- Retention and disposal in accordance with privacy laws

### 15.4 Data Protection and Privacy
- Compliance with applicable data protection regulations (GDPR, CCPA, etc. where applicable)
- Employee consent for data collection and processing
- Data breach response plan
- Regular privacy training for employees handling sensitive information
