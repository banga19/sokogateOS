# Founder Agreement

## Between:
- **Bangali Fofana**, Chief Technology Officer (CTO)
- **Amadou Balde**, Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

## Effective Date: June 13, 2026

## Recitals
Whereas, the Founders wish to establish sokogateOS (the "Company") and desire to set forth their rights and obligations as Founders of the Company.

## Agreement

### 1. Company Formation
The Founders agree to form a company under the name "sokogateOS" or such other name as may be agreed upon.

### 2. Equity Ownership
Initial equity ownership shall be allocated as follows:
- Bangali Fofana: 50%
- Amadou Balde: 50%

### 3. Roles and Responsibilities
- **Bangali Fofana (CTO)**: Responsible for all technical aspects, including product development, architecture, engineering team leadership, and technology strategy.
- **Amadou Balde (CEO)**: Responsible for overall business strategy, operations, fundraising, sales, marketing, and external relations.

### 4. Vesting Schedule
All Founder equity shall be subject to a 4-year vesting schedule with a 1-year cliff:
- 25% vests after 12 months
- Remaining 75% vests monthly over the subsequent 36 months

### 5. Intellectual Property Assignment
All intellectual property related to the Company's business, whether conceived before or after the Effective Date, shall be assigned to the Company.

### 6. Confidentiality
Founders agree to keep confidential all proprietary information of the Company.

### 7. Non-Compete
During their involvement with the Company and for one (1) year thereafter, Founders shall not engage in any business that competes with the Company.

### 8. Founder Departure
In the event of a Founder's departure:
- Voluntary departure after vesting period: Founder keeps vested shares
- Voluntary departure before vesting period: Founder forfeits unvested shares
- Termination for cause: Founder may forfeit all shares (vested and unvested) as determined by Board
- Termination without cause: Founder keeps vested shares

### 9. Decision Making
Major decisions requiring Founder approval include:
- Amending the certificate of incorporation or bylaws
- Approving annual budget
- Authorizing equity financing
- Hiring/firing C-level executives
- Acquisitions or divestitures
- Changes to Founder roles or responsibilities

### 10. Dispute Resolution
Any disputes arising under this Agreement shall be resolved through good-faith negotiation, followed by mediation if necessary, and finally binding arbitration in [JURISDICTION].

### 11. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of [GOVERNING LAW JURISDICTION].

### 12. Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the Founders with respect to the subject matter herein.
