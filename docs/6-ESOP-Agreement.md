# Employee Stock Option Plan (ESOP) Agreement

## sokogateOS Employee Stock Option Plan
### Effective Date: June 13, 2026

## Article 1: Purpose
The purpose of this Employee Stock Option Plan ("Plan") is to:
- Attract and retain superior personnel for the Company
- Provide additional incentives to employees, directors, and consultants
- Promote the success of the Company's business
- Align the interests of plan participants with shareholders

## Article 2: Definitions
- **"Administrator"**: The Board of Directors or its designated committee
- **"Affiliate"**: Any entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with the Company
- **"Award"**: Any stock option, stock appreciation right, restricted stock, or other equity-based award granted under the Plan
- **"Change of Control"**: As defined in Section 11
- **"Code"**: Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended
- **"Committee"**: Compensation Committee of the Board
- **"Company"**: sokogateOS
- **"Disability"**: Permanent and total disability as determined by the Company
- **"Fair Market Value (FMV)"**: As determined in good faith by the Administrator
- **"Grant Date"**: Date an Award is authorized by the Administrator
- **"Option"**: Right to purchase a share of Common Stock under the Plan
- **"Optionee"**: Person to whom an Option is granted
- **"Exercise Price"**: Price per share at which shares may be purchased upon exercise of an Option
- **"Expiration Date"**: Date on which an Option expires
- **"Plan"**: This Employee Stock Option Plan
- **"Participant"**: Eligible person selected to receive an Award under the Plan
- **"Restricted Stock"**: Shares of Common Stock subject to restrictions and/or a risk of forfeiture
- **"Stock Appreciation Right (SAR)"**: Right to receive payment equal to the appreciation in FMV of one share
- **"Termination of Service"**: Date an individual ceases to provide services to the Company

## Article 3: Administration
### 3.1 Authority
The Plan shall be administered by the Administrator, which shall have the power to:
- Select Participants
- Determine types and sizes of Awards
- Establish vesting schedules and restrictions
- Interpret the Plan and establish rules
- Make all other determinations necessary for Plan administration

### 3.2 Delegation
The Administrator may delegate responsibilities to officers of the Company, provided such delegation is documented.

## Article 4: Shares Subject to Plan
### 4.1 Share Reserve
The maximum number of shares available for issuance under the Plan is **2,000,000** shares of Common Stock.

### 4.2 Share Types
Shares issued under the Plan shall be authorized but unissued or reacquired shares of Common Stock.

### 4.3 Share Adjustment
In the event of stock dividends, stock splits, or similar events, the Administrator shall make proportional adjustments to:
- Number and kind of shares available for issuation
- Number and kind of shares subject to outstanding Awards
- Exercise prices and grant prices

## Article 5: Eligibility
The following individuals are eligible to participate in the Plan:
- Employees of the Company or its Affiliates
- Directors of the Company
- Consultants and advisors who provide bona fide services to the Company

## Article 6: Grant of Awards
### 6.1 Grant Procedure
Awards shall be granted by written notice from the Administrator specifying:
- Participant's name
- Type and number of shares subject to Award
- Grant Date
- Exercise Price (for Options)
- Vesting schedule
- Expiration Date
- Any special terms or conditions

### 6.2 Option Specifics
For stock Options granted under the Plan:
- **Exercise Price**: Shall not be less than 100% of FMV on Grant Date
- **Term**: Maximum 10 years from Grant Date
- **Vesting**: Determined by Administrator (typically 4 years with 1-year cliff)
- **Exercise**: Options may be exercised in whole or in part during vesting period if early exercise permitted

### 6.3 Consideration
Participants shall pay consideration for Awards as determined by the Administrator, which may include:
- Cash payment
- Prior services rendered
- Future services to be rendered
- Other property

## Article 7: Vesting and Exercise
### 7.1 Vesting Schedule
Unless otherwise determined by the Administrator, Awards shall vest according to:
- **Standard Vesting**: 25% after 12 months (1-year cliff), then 6.25% per quarter thereafter
- **Alternative Schedules**: May be established by Administrator based on role and circumstances
- **Performance-Based Vesting**: May be implemented for certain Awards

### 7.2 Exercise of Options
- **Exercise Period**: Options may be exercised during the term, subject to vesting
- **Exercise Notice**: Written notice to Company specifying number of shares to be exercised
- **Payment**: Exercise Price payable in cash or other acceptable consideration
- **Delivery**: Shares delivered upon receipt of full Exercise Price
- **Early Exercise**: Permitted if specified in Award agreement (subject to repurchase rights)

### 7.3 Vesting Acceleration
Awards may accelerate upon:
- **Change of Control**: Single or double trigger as determined by Administrator
- **Termination Without Cause**: As specified in Award agreement or employment contract
- **Death or Disability**: Immediate or accelerated vesting as determined by Administrator
- **Other Events**: As specified in Award agreement

## Article 8: Termination of Service
### 8.1 Effect on Options
Upon a Participant's Termination of Service:
- **Vested Options**: May be exercised within Post-Termination Exercise Period (PTEP)
- **Unvested Options**: Forfeited immediately, except as otherwise determined by Administrator
- **Exercise Price**: Remains unchanged from Grant Date

### 8.2 Post-Termination Exercise Periods
Standard PTEPs unless otherwise specified in Award agreement:
- **Voluntary Resignation**: 90 days
- **Termination Without Cause**: 90 days
- **Termination for Cause**: 0 days (immediate forfeiture)
- **Death**: 1 year
- **Disability**: 1 year
- **Retirement**: As determined by Administrator

### 8.3 Effect on Other Awards
Treatment of restricted stock, SARs, and other Awards upon Termination of Service shall be specified in the Award agreement.

## Article 9: Transferability
### 9.1 General Rule
Awards are not transferable except by will or laws of descent and distribution.

### 9.2 Exceptions
The Administrator may permit transfers to:
- Family members
- Trusts for estate planning purposes
- Other Permitted Transferees as defined in Company agreements
- Subject to Company approval and applicable securities laws

## Article 10: Withholding Taxes
The Company shall have the right to:
- Withhold applicable federal, state, and local taxes
- Require Participants to satisfy tax withholding obligations
- Withhold shares upon exercise to cover tax liability (net exercise)
- Require cash payment for tax withholding

## Article 11: Change of Control
### 11.1 Definition
A "Change of Control" occurs upon:
- Sale of substantially all assets
- Merger where Company is not survivor
- Shareholders approve plan of liquidation or dissolution
- Any person or group acquires >50% of voting power
- Board majority changes due to contested election

### 11.2 Effect on Awards
Upon a Change of Control:
- **Assumption**: Awards may be assumed or substituted by surviving entity
- **Acceleration**: Vesting may accelerate per Award agreement (single/double trigger)
- **Cancellation**: Awards with exercise price above FMV may be cancelled for cash payment
- **Continuation**: Awards may continue under same terms if not assumed

## Article 12: Amendment and Termination
### 12.1 Amendment
The Administrator may amend the Plan at any time, provided:
- No amendment impairs rights of existing Participants without consent
- Shareholder approval obtained as required by law or stock exchange rules
- No amendment increases shares available without shareholder approval (if required)

### 12.2 Termination
The Plan may be terminated by the Administrator at any time, provided:
- Termination does not impair outstanding Awards
- Already granted Awards remain subject to Plan terms
- Notice of termination provided to Participants

## Article 13: Data Collection and Privacy
The Company may collect and use Participant data for:
- Plan administration
- Legal and regulatory compliance
- Communication regarding Plan benefits
- Such data shall be handled per Company privacy policy and applicable laws

## Article 14: Governing Law
This Plan shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of [GOVERNING LAW JURISDICTION], without regard to conflict of laws principles.

## Article 15: Severability
If any provision of this Plan is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

## Article 16: No Entitlement to Employment
Participation in the Plan does not create an entitlement to continued employment or service.

## Article 17: Legends and Restrictions
Share certificates and Award agreements shall bear appropriate legends regarding:
- Transfer restrictions
- Securities law exemptions
- Company's right of first refusal
- Other applicable restrictions
