# Legal Compliance Documentation

## sokogateOS
### Effective Date: June 13, 2026

## Overview
This document outlines the legal compliance framework for sokogateOS, detailing our adherence to applicable laws, regulations, and industry standards across all jurisdictions where we operate.

## 1. Applicable Laws and Regulations
sokogateOS complies with the following legal frameworks:

### 1.1 Data Protection and Privacy
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) - European Union
- California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) - California, USA
- Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) - Canada
- Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD) - Brazil
- Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) - South Africa
- Various state-level privacy laws in the United States

### 1.2 Financial Regulations
- Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS)
- Anti-Money Laundering (AML) regulations
- Know Your Customer (KYC) requirements
- Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) regulations (where applicable)
- Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) guidelines (UK)

### 1.3 Consumer Protection
- Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Act - USA
- Consumer Rights Directive - European Union
- Australian Consumer Law
- Various consumer protection statutes globally

### 1.4 Employment and Labor Laws
- Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) - USA
- Title VII of the Civil Rights Act - USA
- Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) - USA
- Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) - USA
- Equivalent labor laws in all operating jurisdictions
- Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) standards

### 1.5 Intellectual Property
- Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) - USA
- Copyright, Designs and Patents Act - UK
- Berne Convention for the Protection of Literary and Artistic Works
- Paris Convention for the Protection of Industrial Property
- Patent Cooperation Treaty (PCT)
- Various trademark laws globally

### 1.6 Cybersecurity and Technology
- National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) Cybersecurity Framework
- ISO/IEC 27001 Information Security Management
- Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA) - USA
- Network and Information Systems (NIS) Directive - EU
- Various cybersecurity laws and regulations
